About Digira Esports

Building Nepal's competitive gaming future, one tournament at a time

Our Story

Digira Esports operates as a dedicated wing under Digira Incorporation, founded by Digira's four founding members with a shared vision to reshape Nepal's competitive gaming landscape.

Our mission is to help establish esports as a recognized and legitimate competitive sport in Nepal. By developing structured tournament environments, professional broadcast productions, and sustainable community pipelines, Digira Esports creates meaningful opportunities for players, content creators, tournament organizers, and the wider gaming community.

From grassroots scrims to flagship championship stages, we are committed to building a stronger, professional esports culture and competitive ecosystem across Nepal.
