04 // INTEL & Q&A

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Digira Esports is Nepal's premier youth-led esports organization based in Kathmandu. We host high-stakes tournaments, weekly community scrims, and broadcast-level competitive gaming events for Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB), Free Fire, PUBG Mobile, and Valorant.

Our main operations are headquartered in Kathmandu, Nepal, with online scrims and LAN events held across major cities in Nepal.

We organize official tournaments and scrims for Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB), Free Fire, PUBG Mobile, and Valorant, offering cash prize pools, trophies, and pathway opportunities for competitive players.

Players can register for upcoming tournaments directly through our website or join our official Discord Community Server for daily scrim announcements and squad recruitment.

We offer Title (Gold), Event (Silver), and Supporting (Bronze) sponsorship tiers tailored to brand activation, broadcast overlay placement, and direct engagement with Nepal's youth demographic. Reach us at digiraesports@gmail.com.
